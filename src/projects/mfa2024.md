Deriving from the flowers and plants native to Richmond, I documented their weekly blossoming and layered them with hardworking digital ants made in Blender. Additionally, I also developed a micro-site using p5js and HTML/CSS for the event information and artist directory. <br/><br/>The 2024 MFA Thesis Exhibition featured 33 outstanding emerging artists and designers.
                Representing eight departments, these students’ ambitious and challenging research pushes beyond
                traditional disciplines to shape what is new and next in visual and material culture. <br/><br/>Big thanks to Shanice Aga, Ryan Sprowl, Chase Westfall and Lauren Thorson.
