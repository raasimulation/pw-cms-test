Polyphonic Shimmering is a collection of writing that shimmers and hums, playing with duration and pacing and expanding language beyond its conventional encasements. The 12 pieces that make up the publication allow us to see what their authors see, both visual and textual, but mostly through language that blurs the distinction between the two. The book and accompanying website were authored, designed, edited, and produced by the MFA students over the course of the semester from <a href="https://megmiller.world/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">Meg Miller</a>’s seminar. <br/><br/>Website designed and coded by me, <a href="https://tsng-khai.com/index.html" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">khai tsng</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/weitong_sun/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">Weitong Sun</a><br/><br/>Polyphonic Shimmering was featured in Kristoffer Tjalve’s <a href="https://www.naiveweekly.com/p/emily-and-josh?publication_id=3348&amp;post_id=144745842&amp;isFreemail=true&amp;r=txpg&amp;triedRedirect=true" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">Naive Weekly</a>  newsletter and Caitlin Dewey’s <a href="https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com/p/703-link-rot-glue-pizza-and-cool" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">Links</a> newsletter.
