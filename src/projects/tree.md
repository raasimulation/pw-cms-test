"what i found under tree" (2023) reimagined themes of queer archive. The private corner
                facilitates an invitation to glance through someone's private computer and find diary entries from an
                unspecified individual about the trees and the sea. Specific contextual clues, from tree types in past
                neighborhoods to online collective representation of the Caspian sea carry themselves throughout the
                chapters of the pseudo-native experience. The experience doesn't reward the person with the secrets of
                the diary but instead expects an exchange of input, be it text, drawing or sound, in order to open up
                and share.
