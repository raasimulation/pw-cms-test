Mobilizing Citizenship (MC) was an educational and research project held at Kunsthall
                Stavanger between 2018 and 2024, which invited both Norwegian and international artists as well as
                designers to collaborate with young people aged 12 to 16. This toolkit captures the knowledge,
                experiences, and reflections developed over the course of the project. <br/><br/>Website designed and
                coded by me and <a href="https://tsng-khai.com/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">khai tsng</a><br/>Art direction by <a href="http://nylondip.com/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">nicole
