A webcam is a window. A screen is a window. A hole is a window. Using electronic objects
                as a mode of connection to the human body, “three aligned windows: say hi” explores the notions of
                digital intimacy, faux distance, permeated vision, and physical touch. Navigation of the body through
                the objects is transmitted through a confusing and laggy network of unreliable cables. Two rooms subvert
                the connection by not presenting what is wanted, keeping the curiosity unsatisfied but entertained.
