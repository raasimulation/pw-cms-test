Don't Hold The Ladder Horizontally is an anthology book that archives writings, research and collaborative notes from first-year VCUarts Graphic Design MFA students. Writings of personal practice, reflections connect through inserts that contain feedback notes from the previous person to the next. <br/><br/> Designed by me, <a href="https://ayakhalife.com/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">aya khalife</a>, <a href="https://molly-garrett.com/" style="text-decoration: underline magenta;" target="_blank">Molly Garrett</a>
